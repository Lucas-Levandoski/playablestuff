The Castle: Circular TD

Android · Strategy / Tower Defense · English and Português (Brasil)

Your castle stands at the center of the map, and enemies march on it from every side. Build towers on fixed slots around the walls, upgrade them and the castle itself, and pick powerful cards between waves to survive as long as you can.

  • Pixel-art tower defense made for one-handed portrait play
  • Upgrade damage, attack speed, range and health on every tower and on the castle
  • Enemies attack the nearest structure, so where you build matters
  • Collect cards and crystals that carry over between runs
  • Free to play, with optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases
