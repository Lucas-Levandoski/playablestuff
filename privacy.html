Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Ler em Português

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayableStuff ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile games, including The Castle: Circular TD (Android package com.playablestuff.thecastle) (the "Game"). By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress (crystals, cards, settings, language) is saved only on your device. We do not run game servers and do not receive this data.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. These partners collect device information, including the Advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores game progress and preferences locally on your device (for example: crystals, unlocked cards, discovered enemies, account progress, language and your ad privacy choice). This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android Settings.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) and Unity Ads to offer optional rewarded videos (for example, to boost the crystals from a run). Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. To serve, cap, measure and prevent fraud in ads, these services may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, app version, network type);
  • Approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address;
  • Ad interaction data (ads viewed, clicked, completed) and diagnostics/crash data related to the ad SDK.

This information is collected and processed by Unity under their own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you personally.

Your ad choices

The first time you open the Game, it asks whether you allow personalized ads. If you choose No, thanks, ads are still available for rewards but are not personalized, and the ad partners are told not to sell or share your data for targeted advertising. You can change this at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy in the Game. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as crystal packs, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads) are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. The Game only receives a purchase confirmation so it can deliver the item and restore non-consumable purchases. We do not see or store your card or billing details.

5. Data we share

We do not sell personal information. The only third parties that receive data from the Game are those listed above:

PartnerPurposeData
Unity LevelPlay / Unity AdsShowing and measuring rewarded ads, fraud preventionAdvertising ID, device and app info, coarse location (IP), ad interactions, diagnostics

We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Security and retention

Data sent by the Game to our partners is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data held by Unity or Google is kept according to their policies.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the LGPD in Brazil, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

  • Delete game data: uninstall the Game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → The Castle → Storage → Clear data. Because there are no accounts, this removes all data we are responsible for.
  • Ads data: change your choice in Settings → Ad Privacy, reset your Advertising ID, or contact Unity through their privacy policy.
  • Questions or requests: email us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes (for example, if we add new ad partners). The latest version will always be on this page with a new effective date.
